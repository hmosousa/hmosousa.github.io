My research interest is in artificial intelligence, mainly focused on natural language processing. During my PhD I have explored the temporal reasoning capabilities of language models. That is, how do language models "understand" and "manipulate" temporal information. Besides that, I am a reinforcement learning aficionado despite all its challenging and complex features. My bachelor's is in Physics and my master's is in Applied Mathematics.
